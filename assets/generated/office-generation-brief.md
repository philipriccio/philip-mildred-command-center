# Severance-Style Isometric Office - Image Generation Brief

## Style Direction
Pixel art isometric office interior in the style of **Severance** (Apple TV series). Slightly eerie, hyper-clean corporate workspace with sterile elegance.

## Visual Language
- Clean white/off-white walls with subtle corporate sterility
- Light-colored floor with visible grid/tile pattern suggesting depth
- Soft ambient lighting, no harsh shadows

- 16:9 aspect ratio
