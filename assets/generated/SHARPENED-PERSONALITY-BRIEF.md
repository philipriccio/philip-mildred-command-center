## THE FOUR DESKS — SHARPENED DEFINITIONS

### DESK 1: MILDRED — LEADERSHIP
**Position:** Top-right, near window/filing cabinet
**Energy:** Elegant, orderly, commanding, refined

**Visual Cues (Minimal but Premium):**
- ONE executive chair — leather, high-back, authoritative silhouette
- Dual monitors — matching, thin bezels, perfectly aligned
- Desk surface: almost empty — just a single quality item (e.g., one elegant pen, one small leather notebook)
- ONE sophisticated plant: single fiddle-leaf fig or orchid in simple ceramic pot
- NO clutter, NO stacks, NO papers visible
- The desk of someone who is in control

**What to avoid:** Multiple plants, busy organizers, scattered items, "lived-in" feel

---

### DESK 2: DEV — BUILDER
**Position:** Bottom-left, near door
**Energy:** Technical, capable, functional, organized power

**Visual Cues (Purposeful Tools):**
- Solid ergonomic chair — functional, not flashy
- Multiple screens — one large main monitor + laptop on stand beside it (clear multi-device setup)
- Mechanical keyboard — visible, distinctive (this is the key cue)
- ONE stack of 2-3 technical books — neatly arranged, spines readable as tech books
- Small robot figurine OR retro computing item — one personal touch
- Cable management visible — organized, not chaotic

**What to avoid:** Messy cables, random clutter, "chaotic coder" stereotype, too many personal items

---

### DESK 3: CLAIRE — CREATIVE DIRECTOR
**Position:** Bottom-center, foreground
**Energy:** Curated visual taste, aesthetic discernment, creative authority

**Visual Cues (Visual References, Not Clutter):**
- Design-conscious chair — subtle flair (distinctive but not flashy)
- Drawing tablet visible — Wacom-style, clearly a creative tool
- Small curated reference board — 3-4 images pinned, arranged with intention (NOT a messy collage)
- ONE design book — lying flat, cover visible
- ONE small aesthetic object — small sculpture, interesting rock, or design object
- Clean surface — creative but controlled

**What to avoid:** Mood board with too many images, scattered papers, "artist mess" stereotype, cluttered supplies

---

### DESK 4: RESERVED — FUTURE AGENT
**Position:** Right side, near clock
**Energy:** Intentionally empty, holding space, ready but unclaimed

**Visual Cues (Presence Through Absence):**
- Basic chair — simple, functional, positioned invitingly
- Clean desk surface — completely empty OR just a single basic monitor (unpowered, blank screen)
- NO personal items
- NO plants
- NO decorations
- The visual message: "this space is prepared for someone"

**What to avoid:** Making it look abandoned, forgotten, or like storage — it should feel intentionally held
